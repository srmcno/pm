Stocks Desk

Intraday long/short trading of digital-asset proxy equities (MSTR, COIN, IBIT, ETHA and peers) against their crypto reference prices, which trade around the clock and reprice ahead of the equity tape. Positions open on dislocation against the trailing half hour of driver movement, close on reversion, and are flattened before the close. Paper account; live execution ships disarmed.

Fills are simulated at quote ± half-spread ± slippage. Betas are fitted daily from 60 days of closes and instruments whose driver explains under 25% of daily variance are excluded. The desk halts itself for the day past a 4% drawdown, requires quotes fresher than 20 seconds to enter, and holds nothing overnight. Live execution requires an Alpaca account, explicit arming flags, and is subject to the pattern day trader rule below $25,000 account equity.
