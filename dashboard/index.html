How a directional trade qualifies

Evidence first100 comparable outcomes, 50 in the price band and 30 earlier forecasts. The model must beat their recorded market-price baseline.

Pay for the whole tradeDisplayed depth, current fees and 1¢ slippage per contract. Conservative probability must exceed acquisition cost by at least 3¢.

Keep losses boundedAt most 2% of account equity per event, 10% open exposure and two qualifying scans. Entries stop after 10% drawdown.

Wait for the actual outcomePositions settle from official final payouts. A near-certain price is not a resolved winner.
