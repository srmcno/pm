OUTCOMES FIRST

The edge has to survive the costs.

The current model is a prospective calibration experiment. It records forecasts before outcomes are known and keeps cash idle until its evidence checks pass.

01

Learn from comparable events

Keep venues separate. Group observations by recurring series, time until the entry cutoff, and 10-point price band. Record one contract per event so sibling bets do not inflate the sample.

100 cohort outcomes · 50 in the price band
02

Beat the market-price baseline

Store each prediction before resolution. Compare its Brier score with the original midpoint on at least 30 later-resolved forecasts. A model must improve the score by more than 0.002.

No backdated labels or imported win claims
03

Price the uncertainty

Use a 99% Wilson lower bound, then subtract another 10 points for variation within the price band. Buy only if that conservative probability exceeds the complete acquisition cost by at least 3¢ per contract.

Book depth + fees + 1¢ slippage per contract
04

Size small and verify twice

Use quarter Kelly, capped at 2% of account equity per event and 10% total open exposure. Require two qualifying scans, quotes no older than 90 seconds, and a spread of 5¢ or less. Halt after 10% peak drawdown.

Hold to official settlement · no leverage
These are conservative research thresholds, not proof of profitability. A 99% interval is not a 99% chance of winning. Repeated testing and changing market conditions can still produce false signals. There may be long periods with no qualifying bet.

THE PLAYBOOK

01 Volume breakout

A completed hourly close above the previous 20-hour high, 1.5× relative volume, and a rising 20/50-hour trend. No entry after price runs more than 0.75 ATR beyond the signal.

02 Trend reclaim

A close back above the 20-hour EMA after a pullback, with the 20-hour EMA above the 50-hour EMA and at least 1.1× volume. A 2 ATR stop and 3R price target define the initial plan.

03 Know when to stop

The shared paper account caps combined open risk at 3%, pauses new entries after a 5% UTC-day loss, and halts at 15% peak drawdown. Gaps can exceed a planned stop. No leverage or averaging down.
