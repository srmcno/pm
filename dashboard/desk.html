Evidence

Every registered desk, scored on data it never saw during fitting. Walk-forward figures use fixed parameters, so no result here is the best of a search, and each desk is replayed at its own capital floor. A desk trades only when the statistic is validated and the author's declared status agrees; a marginal desk runs only when named explicitly in the config file, and anything else stays off and says why.

Allocation

What the account is actually permitted to run right now. A desk is refused when the account sits below its measured capital floor — below that line the per-day regulatory fee floor consumes the edge rather than merely reducing it.

Risk

Live limits. The turnover budget is the important one: the pattern-day-trader rule was repealed in June 2026, and it had been the main thing stopping a small account from grinding itself away on spread. The brake is now internal.
