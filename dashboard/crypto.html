Why the live bot follows this rule

Coinbase daily bars, January 2024 to September 2026, with maker-entry and taker-exit costs. Holding BTC and ETH only while each close stays above its 100-day average (enter +2%, exit −2%):

+118% · max drawdown −36%BTC/ETH daily trend, historical study
+99% · max drawdown −53%BTC buy-and-hold, same period

The trend rule was positive in each third of the sample, and nearby settings were also positive. Many short-horizon altcoin strategies lost 70–95% over the same period without a market filter. A backtest is not a forecast; the paper mirror above starts from its own first cycle and never copies these historical returns.

Paper mirror rules and costs
SignalCompleted UTC daily closes only. Enter when the close is above the 100-day simple average + 2%; exit when it falls below the average − 2%.
SizeAbout 49% of paper equity per coin while long, so both coins together stay under 100%.
0.50% maker entryPaper assumes the worker's resting buy at the best bid fills at that bid. A real resting order may fill later, at another price, or not at all.
0.90% taker exit + 0.10%Trend exits walk the observed bids with the taker fee and an extra slippage reserve.
Crash stop at 80%Modeled like the worker's native stop: triggered at 80% of the entry signal close, filled no better than 95% of the trigger, from observed books or daily lows.
Take-profit at 160%Filled at the target, charged as taker. If a stop and target could both apply, the stop is assumed.

One entry per coin per completed daily bar, and never on a bar that closed before the last exit. Failed reads keep the earlier good reading with its original time and never trigger a paper trade. Equity and the BTC buy-and-hold benchmark are both marked at liquidation value after exit costs. Cycles are scheduled about every five minutes, best effort; they are not continuous quotes or continuous protection.

Costs, risk limits and retirement rules
0.90% takerCurrent assumption for new fills. Older entries and exits retain their recorded fees; historical totals may contain 0.60% fees.
0.50% makerA paper reference assumption, not your verified account tier, but not credited to these strategies.
Spread + depthActual public bid/ask levels are walked for the simulated quantity. Thin books can reduce or eliminate a trade.
+0.10% slippage reserveAdditional adverse execution reserve is charged on each side after the walked book.

No funding rate, margin interest or borrowing is modeled because the tournament is long-only spot. No network or withdrawal fee is charged because the paper accounts never transfer assets on-chain. Each position uses at most 20% of strategy equity, 60% total deployed capital and 1% planned stop risk. New entries pause after a 3% daily loss or 10% peak drawdown.

A strategy retires only after at least 30 closed trades spanning 14 days when the latest 30 trades are net negative, profit factor is below one, and each consecutive block of 10 trades lost money. Retirement cannot automatically restart or reset a ledger. Existing positions remain exit-only.
