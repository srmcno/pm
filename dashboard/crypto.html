Costs, risk limits and retirement rules
0.90% takerCharged on every modeled entry and exit. The strategies use taker costs so they do not assume a passive order fills.
0.50% makerRecorded as the current U.S. entry-level reference rate, but not credited to these strategies.
Spread + depthActual public bid/ask levels are walked for the simulated quantity. Thin books can reduce or eliminate a trade.
+0.10% slippage reserveAdditional adverse execution reserve is charged on each side after the walked book.

No funding rate, margin interest or borrowing is modeled because the tournament is long-only spot. No network or withdrawal fee is charged because the paper accounts never transfer assets on-chain. Each position uses at most 20% of strategy equity, 60% total deployed capital and 1% planned stop risk. New entries pause after a 3% daily loss or 10% peak drawdown.

A strategy retires only after at least 30 closed trades spanning 14 days when the latest 30 trades are net negative, profit factor is below one, and each consecutive block of 10 trades lost money. Retirement cannot automatically restart or reset a ledger. Existing positions remain exit-only.
