Costs, risk limits and retirement rules

The model charges 0.60% fees plus 0.10% additional slippage on each side, in addition to the actual bid/ask spread and walked depth. These are simulation assumptions, not verified account fees. Each position uses at most 20% of its strategy equity, 60% total deployed capital and 1% planned stop risk. Gaps can exceed that planned risk. New entries pause after a 3% daily loss or 10% peak drawdown.

A strategy retires after at least 30 closed trades spanning 14 days when the latest 30 trades and each consecutive block of 10 are net negative. Retirement cannot automatically restart or reset a ledger. Existing positions still exit. Falling-price signals mean selling held coins or staying in cash; no short spot positions or leverage are fabricated.
