    Costs are part of the result

    What the base model charges

    • 2 basis points of half-spread and 5 of adverse slippage on each side. A basis point is 0.01%.
    • September 2026 SEC, FINRA TAF and CAT fees, aggregated by type daily and rounded up to cents.
    • Entry, rebalance and terminal liquidation costs. Fund expenses are already reflected in prices.

    What depends on your setup

    • Base assumes direct-retail commission-free USD trading, free data and no ongoing operating charge.
    • No borrowing, shorts, FX conversions or funding transfers. Uninvested cash earns zero.
    • Returns are before personal taxes. The 25% positive-year haircut is illustrative and does not model tax lots, losses or wash sales.

    Thirty days is an assumed distribution payment delay, not a guaranteed payment date. The 60-day stress retains the same rules. Terminal value includes any remaining receivables, which are not yet spendable cash.

      The fixed rule

      SPY, EFA, IEF, GLD and VNQ each receive a 20% sleeve when their total-return index is above its ten-month average, including the latest completed month. Otherwise that sleeve stays in cash. Targets are checked monthly, using completed information, and filled at the next eligible session's opening-price proxy.

      This is an implementation inspired by published tactical allocation research and trend-following evidence. Those studies do not validate this exact fund universe, execution or account. The current ETFs were selected retrospectively; survival and selection bias remain.
